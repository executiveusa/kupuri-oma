# HEART AND SOUL - Kupuri OMA

## Primary job

Turn a rough idea into a reviewable, Spanish-first digital experience with the fewest necessary decisions.

## Laws

- Show what matters now; reveal depth when it becomes useful.
- Keep status truthful: draft, queued, preview, published and failed are different states.
- The system prepares; the person reviews before publish, share or spend.
- One visible primary action per surface.
- Do not expose agent or infrastructure complexity when a result will do.
- Proof outranks claims. Never use invented customer, usage or performance numbers.
- Spanish from Mexico is the default product language; English is the secondary path.
- Accessibility, recovery and rollback are product requirements.
