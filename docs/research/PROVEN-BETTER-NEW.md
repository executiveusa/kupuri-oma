# PROVEN-BETTER-NEW CARD - Kupuri OMA

**Instinct:** Turn a rough business idea into polished visual communication without needing professional design training.  
**Primary:** Canva - its users include creators, small businesses, nonprofits and teams, the same broad non-designer and small-team audience OMA names. Canva reported 260M monthly users and $3.5B revenue in 2025.

## Proven - think about

- **Acquisition / Canva:** Does OMA keep a genuinely useful free first result before asking for payment, rather than using an unwired "free" registration path?
- **Core loop / Canva:** Can a user move from intent to editable output to reuse in one visible loop? OMA currently promises prompt-to-site while several execution paths are placeholders.
- **Retention / Canva:** What reusable asset compounds - projects, brand context, templates or remix history - and which one is canonical rather than split across SQL, graph and local state?
- **Monetization / Canva + Framer:** Can the paid boundary be attached to clear capacity and collaboration, while preview remains useful? Test a private pricing interview before billing work.
- **Distribution / Canva:** Is Spanish-first education and creator community a real acquisition loop, not only localized copy?
- **Moat / Canva:** Will every completed project improve a reusable LATAM template/style graph with rights and provenance?

## Better - think about

- **Spanish-first outcome:** Would 9-10 of 10 Mexican Canva users prefer native es-MX defaults, MXN price context and bilingual publishing enough to switch for a website job? Test five observed tasks side by side.
- **One-sentence creation:** Can OMA reduce website first-run setup to intent, prepared result and review while Canva/Wix expose more decisions? Time ten cold users to a credible preview.
- **Agent-native reuse:** Do the same brief and approval state survive web, CLI, Claude Code and ChatGPT without translation loss? Contract-test one fixture across all four.
- **Adjacent variant:** Agencies may value a Spanish-first client-brief-to-preview handoff more than individual template remixing. Test with three real agency briefs and willingness to pay.
- **Complaint-driven test:** Users who dislike credit ambiguity need limits stated before generation. Show exact included outcomes, not abstract AI credits, and test comprehension.

## New - think about

- **Graph-backed community:** The graph is unproven as a buying reason and should remain behind search/recommendation, not become the pitch's load-bearing wall.
- **Voice-first build:** Voice can shorten entry but may reduce control for detailed design constraints. Compare completion and correction rates against one text field.
- **3D/cinematic generation:** This is expensive and technically risky. Quarantine it as a premium optional output until quality and unit economics are measured.
- **Multi-agent orchestration:** Users buy the result, not the agent count. Hide orchestration and test whether it improves accepted first previews.
- **ChatGPT distribution:** App-directory reach is unproven for this audience. Stage a read/prepare connector first; do not make store approval the core acquisition plan.

**Graveyard:** No close audience-matched failure with enough primary-source detail was verified in this pass. The absence is not evidence of safety; broad creative suites regularly remove products and features when workflows do not consolidate.  
**Anti-signal:** Existing code volume and internal architecture do not prove stranger demand or willingness to pay.  
**Sharpest tensions:** OMA's differentiated generation is currently less wired than its marketing; a broad Canva-like suite risks hiding the single paid job; Spanish-first positioning is promising but must produce a materially faster accepted website, not just translated UI.
