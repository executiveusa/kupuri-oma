# Positioning and pricing - Spanish first, English second

## Español

### Unaware - Ana, comerciante independiente

Runs a small food or service business from her phone. She thinks her problem is that posting and updating her site always takes too long. Lead with the finished outcome: "Describe tu negocio. Revisa un sitio listo para adaptar." Do not lead with agents, graphs or MCP.

### Problem aware - Diego, creador bilingüe

Loses hours translating, resizing and rebuilding the same launch across tools. Lead with one Spanish-first project that can produce an English version after review. His proof trigger is a real preview from his own brief.

### Solution aware - Marisol, freelance designer

Already uses Canva, Wix or Framer and wants faster client starts without losing control. Lead with a prepared draft, visible approval states and reusable LATAM references. Her concern is generic AI output and unclear credits.

### Product aware - Luis, líder de agencia pequeña

Has seen OMA and compares workflow, collaboration and pricing. Lead with an agency brief-to-preview handoff, five seats, clear limits and no automatic publishing.

### Most aware - Valeria, early adopter/developer

Wants to connect OMA to Claude Code, a terminal or ChatGPT. Lead with one shared contract across plugin, CLI and MCP, Spanish defaults and approval gates. She needs working examples and honest runtime status.

### Private-preview prices to validate

- Gratis: $0 MXN. Community, three remixes, five previews.
- Pro: $249 MXN/month ($14 USD). Individual creator, 20 remixes, 50 previews, bilingual publishing and basic 3D.
- Studio: $699 MXN/month ($39 USD). Up to five people, higher capacity, advanced outputs and analytics.
- Enterprise: interview-led, no published number yet.

These are staged research prices. Billing is off. Test comprehension, willingness to pay and generation cost before launch.

## English

The awareness ladder is outcome-first: unaware owners buy a credible site, problem-aware creators buy less duplicated work, solution-aware designers compare control and quality, product-aware agencies compare capacity and collaboration, and most-aware developers require real contracts and evidence. The staged tiers are Free, Pro at $14/month, Studio at $39/month, and interview-led Enterprise. Nothing is live or billable.
